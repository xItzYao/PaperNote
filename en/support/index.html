Need a hand?

Check the questions below. If that doesn't solve it, let me know on GitHub Issues.

Report a problem or idea

Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version (in About Me) and steps to reproduce. Issues are public, so please don't attach private notes.

Getting started

Which devices are supported?

iPads with iPadOS 18 or later. There is no iPhone, Mac or Android version.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. Turn on Draw with Finger in the writing settings, though Pencil gives the full experience.

How do I change the language?

Choose it in the library's ⋯ menu, then close and reopen the app.

Import and export

What can I import?

PDF, a zip of PDFs, and PaperNote files. Remove PDF passwords first.

How do I move from another notes app?

Export to PDF there (zip several together) and import it. Existing ink usually becomes page content and may not be editable.

Which export format should I use?

PDF for sharing and printing, or a PaperNote file to keep editing in PaperNote.

Writing and translation

My palm leaves marks.

Check the handedness setting and turn off Draw with Finger so fingers only scroll.

Why can't I translate?

Translation uses iPadOS language packs. Download both languages in Settings → Apps → Translate → Downloaded Languages.

I can't select text in a scan.

Wait for recognition to finish the first time. Blurry or handwritten content may not be recognized.

Backup and restore

How do I back up?

Connect in ⋯ → Cloud Backup (Google Drive). Backups run every 10 minutes and after closing a notebook, or tap Back Up Now.

How do I move to a new iPad?

Tap Back Up Now on the old iPad and wait for it to finish, then connect the same Google account on the new one and tap Restore from Google Drive…

Does restoring overwrite my notes?

No. It only adds notebooks missing from this iPad and never overwrites or deletes existing ones.

What happens to backups after permanent deletion?

The next backup moves them to the Google Drive trash, and the app won't restore them again.

How do I revoke access?

Tap Disconnect on the Cloud Backup screen. To revoke fully, remove PaperNote from your Google Account third-party access.

Keeping data safe

I deleted something by mistake.

Notebooks and folders are in Trash, pages in Pages → Recently Deleted Pages, for 30 days.

What if I delete the app?

Notes on that iPad are deleted with it. Back up and make sure it finished first.
